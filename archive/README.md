## `essay/` (parked 2026-11-25)

Drafts of a critique of Fousek et al. (2024): `v3/` is the last full draft, `v5/` is `v3` reordered with marked
additions, `v4_theory/` and `skeleton_v2/` are earlier stages.

Why it is parked. Its general argument (reductions, backward error and conditioning, identification relative to a
target) now lives in a separate note, where it was audited and repaired. The worked numbers live in this repository
(`doc/sweep_writeup.pdf`, `doc/LOG.md`). Work goes back and forth between those two.

What only the essay holds: the reading of the paper itself. That is the paper's explanatory claim and the standard it
is held to, the objection, the reply on the authors' behalf, the two positions that remain, and the nine cases worked
statistic by statistic. Its symbols and its "unbounded condition number" wording were never brought in line with the
note.
