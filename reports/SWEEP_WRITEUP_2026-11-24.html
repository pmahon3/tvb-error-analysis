Which parameters can the summary statistics tell apart?

Draft, machine-written, 2026-11-24. Not yet rewritten in the author's words. Every number is computed from the run files by sim/writeup_sweep.py. All values are mean ± one standard error over independent runs ("chains": same parameters, different random seed and starting state).

What was run

  • Model. 84 regions (hcp-001 connectome), Montbrió–Pazó–Roxin node, η = −5, J = 15, Δ = 1, delays at speed 20, stochastic Heun at step 0.01, noise [σ, 2σ]. These are the settings of the group's companion code; that the paper used them is an inference.
  • Reference. The Virtual Brain 2.10.0 (numba backend) through the group's own code. 96 runs at the paper's working point (G 0.54, σ 0.030), fewer at neighbouring G and at other steps.
  • Port. A JAX rewrite of the same kernel on two GPUs. It reproduces TVB to 2 × 10⁻¹² over 3,000 steps when given TVB's own noise.
  • Sweep. 10,044 port runs of 61,000 time units (first 1,000 discarded): G 0.40–0.70 × σ 0.018–0.054, each point with η, J and Δ nudged up and down, and again at half the step. Four runs per setting.

A comparison of pattern statistics from a handful of runs is dominated by this spread. The first 32 TVB runs alone gave 0.0129 ± 0.0006; the next 64 gave 0.0112 ± 0.0005.

2. Chains are still settling after the discard

Both codes start every region at a rate between 0 and 2. In the first volume after the discard the high-state share is 0.146 (TVB) and 0.126 (port), against 0.057 later. Over the first 20 volumes it averages 0.1036 ± 0.0072 and 0.0968 ± 0.0093. At this working point the effect on whole-record statistics is under 3%. At lower noise (σ ≤ 0.026) near the transition it is not small: the starting state persists much longer there.

  • In the median, one grid step of G (0.01) does what η + 0.012 (0.23%), J + 0.025 (0.16%) or Δ + 0.010 (1%) does.
  • The cosines fall short of 1 by as much as two halves of the runs disagree with each other about a single parameter, so the shortfall is noise. No separation of G from η, J or Δ is seen, and none is ruled out below the noise of four runs.
  • σ is the one parameter the statistics see separately: more noise acts like more coupling below G ≈ 0.56 and like less above it.
  • The step is the least resolved. For the level statistics, halving it acts like a small increase in G.

This was expected from the equations: η, J·r and G × (input from other regions) enter the same equation as a sum. The prediction and its probability (0.8) were written down before the sweep. What the sweep adds is the sizes.

Not shown

  • Per-region statistics and the paper's own fluidity statistic. Not analysed. G weights input by the connectome and η does not, so per-region quantities are where a separation could appear.
  • Switching index. Too noisy at four runs for anything beyond the shift of its peak with η.
  • σ ≤ 0.026 near the transition. Those statistics still carry the decay from the starting state.
  • High G with high σ. 44 of 10,044 runs went non-finite there (grey cells). Whether TVB does the same is untested.
  • TVB across the grid. The maps are the port's. TVB was run at the working point, two neighbouring G values and four steps.
  • Three of the seven statistics (mean rate, high-state occupancy, median high-state share) are nearly one statistic, which makes the cosines high cheaply.
